About

Atwater Estate Vineyards

Established in 1999, Atwater Estate Vineyards is a family-owned winery spread across 80 scenic acres on the eastern shore of Seneca Lake, 7 miles north of Watkins Glen. As a true estate winery, Atwater is intimately connected to every vine they grow — all 19 grape varieties cultivated with precision and genuine care across a microclimate uniquely suited to cool-climate viticulture.

The tasting room features panoramic views of Seneca Lake and a warm, knowledgeable staff who guide visitors through thoughtfully produced wines in a range of styles — from crisp whites and sparkling expressions to serious reds and skin-contact wines. Guided seated tastings available with advance reservation, walk-ins welcome for most visits. The outdoor Vista Bar is one of the most beloved spots on the east shore, and stays open late on Thursday evenings for Atwater After Hours — making Atwater the perfect sunset destination.

The winemaking program embraces variety and technique: neutral barrels, skin and stem inclusion, Pétillant Naturel sparkling, and a rotating cast of whites, reds, and blends that change with each vintage. The Atwater After Hours series — Thursday evenings from 6 to 9 PM — has turned the Vista Bar into one of the most vibrant evening destinations on Seneca Lake.

In Our Words

Our Story

Atwater is one of the more complete experiences on the east shore — the wines are genuinely interesting and thoughtfully made, the tasting room staff are knowledgeable without being precious about it, and that view of Seneca Lake is about as good as it gets anywhere on Route 414. The Riesling is a standout. The Pét-Nat is something to seek out.

But what really sets Atwater apart is the Vista Bar in the evening. If you haven't watched the sun set over Seneca Lake from that outdoor bar with a cold glass of something excellent in hand, add it to the list immediately. Thursday After Hours nights have made Atwater one of the most fun places to be on the east shore after 6 PM.

— South East Seneca

An adventurous, evolving portfolio from 19 estate varieties — guided tastings available daily, Atwater After Hours Thursdays until 9 PM.

Ways to Visit

Guided Tasting

5 wines, cracker and cheese sampling, guided by an experienced staff member. Allow 45–60 minutes. Groups of up to 4 without reservation, up to 6–8 with advance booking.

Vista Bar

Outdoor bar serving wine by the glass, cocktails, zero-proof, and small bites. Open daily 11 AM–6 PM. Open late Thursdays until 9 PM. Dogs welcome in the outdoor area.

Atwater After Hours

Every Thursday, 6–9 PM, at the Vista Bar. The best evening experience on Route 414.

Private Groups

Groups of 6–8 can book private tastings by emailing the tasting room. Special events and wine club gatherings also available.

Plan Your Visit

Tips for Visitors

  • Start with the Riesling Lahoma — the vineyard-designated expression is one of the best whites on the east shore.
  • Come back Thursday evening (6–9 PM) for Atwater After Hours — watching the sunset from that outdoor bar is a Finger Lakes must-do.
  • The Thursday Singer-Songwriter series at After Hours (6–9 PM) is low-key and consistently excellent.
  • Reservations recommended for weekends — book at exploretock.com/atwatervineyards.
  • Try the Pét-Nat if it's available — the Zweigelt-Gewürztraminer sparkling is genuinely one of the most fun wines in the region.
  • 5055 State Route 414, Burdett — 7 miles north of Watkins Glen, directly across from J.R. Dill.
